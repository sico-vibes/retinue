# Hire your first employee

An employee is a named coding agent with a role, a personality and its own rules. Retinue comes with a team of ready-made roles, such as a backend engineer, a frontend engineer, a code reviewer and a release manager. You can hire one of them, or make your own.

![The team page with the hire dialog open](/docs/docs-hire-dark.webp)

## Steps

1. Open **Team** from the sidebar.
2. Choose **Hire**. You can also open the plus menu and choose **Hire an employee**.
3. Pick a role from the list. Each one shows what it is good at, so choose the one that fits the work.
4. Give the employee a name, or keep the suggested one.
5. Choose the **coding agent** it runs on, such as Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode. The agents Retinue found on your PC are listed. An agent you have not signed in to yet may need a sign-in before it works.
6. Choose the **approval mode**. Start with **Ask first**. You can change it later.
7. Choose the project the employee works on, if you have one. See [Projects](/docs/projects/).
8. Check the summary and choose **Hire**.

The employee appears in the sidebar. Select it to open its page, or start a conversation with it right away.

## Good to know

- **Ask first** is the safest choice. The employee asks before it runs a command or changes a file.
- Your choice of coding agent sets the agent for new conversations. Existing conversations keep the agent they started with.
- You can hire the same role more than once, for example two backend engineers on different projects.
- To change an employee later, see [Hiring and editing employees](/docs/hiring-and-editing-employees/).
