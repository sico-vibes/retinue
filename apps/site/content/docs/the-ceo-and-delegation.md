# The CEO and delegation

The CEO is a special employee that helps you run the rest of the team. You talk to it the way you would talk to a manager. It can suggest a team for a project, brief the other employees on their tasks and help when something goes wrong.

![The CEO's conversation](/docs/docs-ceo-dark.webp)

## What the CEO does

- **Staffs a project.** When you start a new project, the CEO can propose the employees it needs and hire them with you.
- **Briefs employees.** A brief names the task, what the employee should not touch, the commands to run and what to report back. You see the brief in the conversation.
- **Helps with failures.** When a turn fails, you can choose **Ask the CEO to troubleshoot**. The CEO opens a conversation with the facts of the failure and suggests what to do next.

## Steps: ask the CEO to staff a project

1. Open the project, or choose **New project** from the plus menu.
2. Describe what you want to build, and ask the CEO to propose a team.
3. Read the proposal. It lists each role and why it is needed.
4. Choose the employees you want to hire. Choose **Skip** if you want to do it yourself.
5. The CEO can then brief the new employees on the work.

## Good to know

- Ask the CEO for what you need. Anything an employee does still follows that employee's approval mode.
- An employee hired into a project is part of that project for the CEO too.
- You can always talk to an employee directly. You do not need to go through the CEO.
