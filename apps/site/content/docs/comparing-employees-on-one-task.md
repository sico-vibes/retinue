# Comparing employees on one task

Sometimes you want a second opinion, or you want to see how two coding agents handle the same problem. Ask several employees at once, and Retinue shows their answers side by side in a comparison.

![A comparison of several answers](/docs/docs-compare-dark.webp)

## Steps

1. Open the plus menu in the sidebar and choose **Ask several employees**. You need at least two employees to do this.
2. Choose the employees you want to ask.
3. Write the question or task.
4. Start it. Each employee answers in its own conversation.
5. Open the **Comparisons** view from the plus menu, or **Compare answers** in a conversation, to see the answers side by side.

## Good to know

- Compare the answers before you choose one. Pick the answer you like, then keep working with that employee in its own conversation.
- Running several employees costs what each one costs on its own coding agent. Check your usage first. See [Usage limits and accounts](/docs/usage-limits-and-accounts/).
