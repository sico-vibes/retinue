# Privacy and your data

Retinue is built to keep your work on your computer. This page explains what it stores, what it never keeps, and the few places where something can leave your PC. Each of those is off unless you turn it on.

## What stays on your PC

- Your employees, projects and conversations are stored locally.
- Your coding agents keep their own sign-ins. Retinue does not copy them.
- Backups are files you create and keep yourself, from Settings, then System, then Backup.

## What Retinue never writes down

- The text of your messages does not appear in logs or in activity lists.
- Your sign-ins and tokens are not written to logs.
- Audio and transcripts from dictation are not saved.

## The exceptions, which you choose

- **Usage bars.** Off by default. If you turn it on, Retinue sends your coding agent's sign-in only to that agent's own usage page. It is not stored.
- **Cloud dictation.** Off by default. If you turn it on, your audio goes to the cloud service, and it uses your Codex sign-in for that one purpose. The on-device engine stays the default.
- **Remote access and linked machines.** Only if you turn them on, and only to devices and machines you approve yourself. See [Your phone and the web version](/docs/your-phone-and-the-web-version/).

## Good to know

- Keep your backups somewhere safe, because they contain your conversations.
- You can turn each exception off at any time, and the data it used stops being sent.
