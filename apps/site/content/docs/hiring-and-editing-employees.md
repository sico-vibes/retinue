# Hiring and editing employees

You can change an employee at any time. Open the employee in the sidebar, then open its settings. The settings are grouped into the same parts described in [How employees work](/docs/how-employees-work/).

![Editing an employee](/docs/docs-team-dark.webp)

## Steps

1. Select the employee in the sidebar.
2. Open its settings from the employee's page.
3. Change what you need: the name, the role or instructions, the coding agent, or the approval mode.
4. Leave the page when you are done. Changes you made are kept.

## What each setting does

- **Name and role.** Shown in the sidebar and in the conversations.
- **Coding agent.** Used by new conversations. Existing conversations keep their own agent.
- **Approval mode.** Sets what the employee may do without asking. See [Approvals and permissions](/docs/approvals-and-permissions/).
- **Works on the computer.** A switch that appears once a computer on your server is shared with employees. With it on, the employee uses that computer as its workplace. See [A cloud computer for your employees](/docs/a-cloud-computer-for-your-employees/).

## Hire another from the same role

To add a second employee with the same role, go back to **Team** and choose **Hire**. The new employee starts with the role's default skills and no conversations.

## Good to know

- Changes to the coding agent or model apply to new conversations only.
- If you change the approval mode to **Full access**, Retinue asks you to confirm first.
