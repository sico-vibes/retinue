# Retinue 0.1.0-beta.51

The first release under the name Retinue. Employees now wear profile photos, can be hired with their own personality and skills, and can answer with interactive views. Opening a conversation no longer sets it working again.

## Before you update

- **Retinue is a new app name.** It installs next to the old app, with its own data folder. Conversations, employees and settings from the old app are not moved over automatically.

## Added

- **Hiring with choices.** Hiring a preset employee opens a dialog where you choose:
  - their name and photo;
  - a personality: ten presets with an example reply, and the text can be edited;
  - the coding agent, model and effort;
  - their skills: a curated set is on by default and a few optional ones are off, each with its own toggle.
  
  An employee can also be hired without a project.
- **A skill library.** 34 open-source skills are bundled with the app, each with its licence and source recorded.
- **Profile photos belong to one employee.** A photo another employee wears is not offered until that employee changes theirs. The photo picker shows the photos a page at a time, with Upload first.
- **Status dots.** Each photo in the sidebar shows whether the employee is online, working, waiting for you, failed, at a usage limit or offline.
- **Interactive replies.** Employees can answer with a view built from cards, numbers, tables, charts, tabs, buttons and short forms. A button or form only fills your message box: nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **Ask the CEO to troubleshoot.** Hover a failed message to open a conversation with the CEO about it. The CEO can propose a GitHub issue, but nothing is posted until you approve the exact text.
- **Haze settings.** Background opacity, haze strength, shade and the haze colour, with a reset.
- **Turn a coding agent off.** In Settings › Coding agents, an agent that is turned off isn't checked at startup, counted in usage or offered to employees.
- **Update arrow.** When an update is ready, a round arrow appears beside your name in the sidebar.

## Changed

- The **+** button opens Hire an employee, Create your own employee and New project. "Hire an employee" also ends the Team list.
- Clicking an employee or a conversation in the sidebar leaves the page you were on, such as settings, tasks or usage.
- Sidebar photos are larger, the coding agent's badge is smaller, and the CEO's star sits beside the name.
- The hire catalogue cards show each role's icon.
- In Settings › Connections, the copy button is a small icon in the corner of the command.
- The Tasks tree view is replaced by a Kanban view that is coming soon.

## Fixed

- Opening a conversation no longer continues interrupted work by itself, and no longer moves the conversation to the top of the list. The time shown is the time of its last message.

## Installation

Download `Retinue_0.1.0-beta.51_x64-setup.exe` and check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show a warning before it runs.
