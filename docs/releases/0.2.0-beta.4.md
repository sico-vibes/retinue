# Retinue 0.2.0-beta.4

Employees can use a browser and the computer on your server, and, if you turn it on, this computer. Bring in conversations from Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, see each employee's task list as it works, and a fresher chat.

## New

- **Employees can use this computer, only if you let them.** Settings › Integrations › Computer has a new switch, off by default. With it on, an employee still has to ask in its conversation, and you choose **Allow for this conversation**, **Once** or **No**. While it works, a bar names the employee with the time left and a red **Stop**, and the companion shows the same. A permission lasts 5 minutes, ends after a minute with nothing done, and never covers a conversation set to Full access or one nobody is watching. Employees read your screen as text with password fields hidden, and no picture of your screen is sent. Password managers, banking, mail and terminals are off limits, and you can add your own.
- **The computer on your server moves.** Employees can take a screenshot there, click, type, press keys and scroll. Screenshots appear in the conversation like a generated image. The computer's card says if it is missing something, and how to install it.
- **A browser on your server's computer.** Employees can open a page, read it, click, type and go back, in Retinue's own browser with none of your sign-ins. They stop and ask you before a sign-in, a verification code, a payment or a post.
- **Import conversations.** New › Import conversations finds your Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode conversations from the last 30 days. Pick the ones you want, then give them to one employee, to the CEO, or let Retinue share them out by project folder, coding agent and role, and change any choice before you confirm. An imported conversation can carry on when its own coding agent still has it; otherwise it is kept to read. Passwords, sign-ins and the agents' private reasoning are never copied.
- **Task lists.** For work with more than a couple of steps, an employee makes a task list and ticks it off as it goes, and you see it under the conversation.
- **Retinue knows itself.** Every employee can read a built-in guide to how Retinue works (employees, projects, conversations, the CEO, every tool), so the CEO can organise work without asking you how. Employees working on your server's computer get a guide to it too.
- **Coloured replies.** Headings, code and the key facts in a reply each get their own colour, and the one or two most important points are highlighted. On an employee's page, **Reply style** lets it add small icons or emoji to tables and lists.
- **Copy a conversation's ID** from its menu, to point Claude Code or another tool at it.

## Changed

- **Opening a conversation no longer moves it in the sidebar.** Lists are ordered by the last message.
- Each conversation row has one **…** menu, also on right-click, instead of buttons over its title. A conversation started by another program through the MCP shows the MCP mark. An employee with a single conversation can be folded.
- **Images:** images made one after another sit in one strip you can scroll. While an image is being made, a placeholder shows where it will appear, and images now sit above the reply that talks about them. Opening an image blurs everything behind it, and you can zoom with the wheel, the buttons or a double-click, and drag to look around.
- A **Back to the latest message** button appears above the composer when you scroll up.
- Opened table rows have more room.
- **One effort control for every coding agent**, with Faster and Smarter ends. At the highest level, sparks rise in the control and behind the composer. Haze draws it in rounded rectangles, and Stone in pills.
- A new working indicator in conversations, a new launch screen with a revealed background and a metallic logo, and the logo at the top left turns to metal when you point at it.
- Settings › Appearance shows the two looks as wide cards. A chosen profile photo is ringed like a chosen look, and the photo page arrows stay put.

## Installation

Download `Retinue_0.2.0-beta.4_x64-setup.exe` and check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Retinue 0.1.0-beta.32 and later update themselves. The installer is not code-signed yet:

- Microsoft Defender may report it as `Program:Win32/Contebrew.A!ml`. This is a false positive from its automatic scanner. To keep the file, open Windows Security › Protection history, select the entry and choose **Allow on device**.
- SmartScreen may then say Windows protected your PC. Choose **More info** › **Run anyway**.
- On a PC with **Smart App Control** turned on, Windows blocks unsigned installers with no way to run them. Retinue can be installed there once releases are code-signed.
- The installer now includes a small open-source driver, `open-computer-use` 1.1.4, used only when you let employees control this computer. Its exact build is pinned and checked when the installer is made.
