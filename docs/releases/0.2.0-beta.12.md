# Retinue 0.2.0-beta.12

Another machine's employees now open like your own, and Haze gets motion and a few fixes.

## New

- **Other devices, tidied and foldable.** The sidebar groups other machines under an "Other devices" label. Each machine folds and unfolds, remembers how you left it, and shows its actions when you hover. A remote employee's avatar carries a small icon for the kind of device it runs on.
- **A remote conversation opens like a local one.** Choosing an employee on another machine fills the conversation pane and the Conversations column, with a chip that names the machine and says whether the link is live.
- **Motion in Haze.** Rows light up where the pointer is, lists and messages rise in a few milliseconds apart, buttons press in slightly, and the Projects and Team switch slides one plate between its options. Switches that are on take your accent colour.
- **Usage, redrawn.** Plan limits are cards with full-width bars, and the bars and chart grow in. The figures settle in.
- **Settings and Tasks** get the same hover light and rise-in.

## Fixed

- **The logo at the top left** no longer turns into a sad-face square when the animated metal paint can't draw. The plain logo shows instead.
- **A running step** ("Running npm test") keeps its icon still. Only the words move.

## Installation

Download `Retinue_0.2.0-beta.12_x64-setup.exe` and check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Retinue 0.1.0-beta.32 and later update themselves. The installer is not code-signed yet:

- Microsoft Defender may report it as `Program:Win32/Contebrew.A!ml`. This is a false positive from its automatic scanner. To keep the file, open Windows Security › Protection history, select the entry and choose **Allow on device**.
- SmartScreen may then say Windows protected your PC. Choose **More info** › **Run anyway**.
- On a PC with **Smart App Control** turned on, Windows may block Retinue, because it is not code-signed. There is no per-app exception; it runs once Smart App Control is off, or once releases are code-signed.
