# The companion window

The companion is a small floating window that stays on top of your other apps. It shows your team at a glance, so you can see who is busy and who needs you without opening Retinue. It starts at the bottom centre of your screen, and you can drag it anywhere you like.

![The companion window in chat](/docs/docs-companion-dark.webp)

## What you can do in the companion

- **See your team.** Each employee's character shows what it is doing: waiting, working or needing you.
- **Chat.** Open the chat view to send a message to an employee without switching to the main window.
- **Answer requests.** When an employee needs permission, the request appears in the companion. Choose to allow it, deny it, or choose **Later**.
- **Dictate.** Use the microphone to speak a message. See [Dictation](/docs/dictation/).

## Steps: move the companion

1. Point at the companion and drag it by its edge.
2. Let go where you want it. It stays there until you move it again.

## Good to know

- The companion uses the same team, conversations and approvals as the main window. Anything you do in one shows up in the other.
- You can keep the companion on a second screen to watch the team while you work in another app.
