# Dictation

Dictation turns your speech into text in the message box and in the companion. You speak, and the words are added to your message. With the default engine, your voice is turned into text on your PC, using a speech model you download once.

## Turn dictation on

1. Open Settings, then Chat, then Dictation.
2. Turn on **Dictation**.
3. If the speech model is not downloaded yet, download it from the same page. It is needed once.

## Use dictation

1. Click the microphone in the message box or in the companion, or press **Ctrl+E**.
2. Speak your message.
3. Click the microphone again to stop. The words are added to the message.
4. Check the text, then send it.

## Choose a speech engine

- **On this PC** is the default. Your voice stays on your computer.
- **Cloud** is an option you must approve. It sends your audio to a cloud service. It uses your Codex sign-in only when you choose it, and only for dictation. If the cloud engine is not available, Retinue falls back to the on-device engine.

## Good to know

- Retinue does not save your audio or transcripts.
- You can turn dictation off at any time in the same page.
- The companion has its own dictation button, so you can speak to an employee without opening the main window.
