# Retinue documentation

Retinue is a Windows app for hiring AI coding agents and working with them as a team. You hire an employee, give it a project and a coding agent it already runs on your computer, such as Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode, and then talk to it the way you would talk to a colleague. Each employee keeps its own conversations, its own working folder and its own rules for what it may do without asking. You can run several of them at once, see who is busy and who needs you, and step in whenever you want. Retinue does not replace the coding agents you already have. It sits on top of them, so the sign-ins, models and subscriptions stay where they are.

## Where to start

1. [Install Retinue](/docs/install-retinue/) on a Windows PC, and let it find the coding agents already installed there.
2. [Hire your first employee](/docs/hire-your-first-employee/) and give it a project.
3. [Have your first conversation](/docs/your-first-conversation/) and see what comes back.
4. Read [How employees work](/docs/how-employees-work/) so you know what an employee is made of.
5. When you have more than one employee, read [The CEO and delegation](/docs/the-ceo-and-delegation/) and [Approvals and permissions](/docs/approvals-and-permissions/).

## Good to know

- Retinue runs on your own computer. Your conversations and files stay there unless you choose to share them.
- Retinue is in beta. Check the release notes for what has changed before you rely on a feature.
- The pages in this section are written for people who use Retinue, not for people who build it.
