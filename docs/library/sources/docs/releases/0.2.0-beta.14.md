# Retinue 0.2.0-beta.14

Professional mode, settled conversations, and Settings as a dialog.

## New

- **Professional mode.** Settings › Appearance › Mode switches between Employees (your team, as before) and Professional: one plain list of chats with provider logos, grouped by project, with tabs above the open chat. It is a view over the same conversations, so nothing is moved or turned off and you can switch back any time. Employees stays the default.
  - Each chat shows its project and its status as a coloured icon: a tick when it is done, a spinner while it works (with a timer of how long it has been working), a ringing bell when it needs you.
  - Chats on your other devices sit in their own groups below, with the device's status before its name.
  - The details panel leads with the chat, its provider and its project, and subagents show their provider's logo.
- **Settled conversations.** A conversation idle for a week, or one you settle from its menu, moves to a Settled shelf at the bottom of the list. Open the shelf to search old conversations by title, project or device, or move one back.

## Changed

- **Settings opens as a dialog** over the app in both looks, with the same pages as before and the close button at its top right.
- **Dropdown menus** have smaller corners.
- **The conversation header** keeps the provider logo next to the project name.

## Fixed

- The "2 finished" line in the details panel was larger than the rest of the panel.

## Installation

Download `Retinue_0.2.0-beta.14_x64-setup.exe` and check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Retinue 0.1.0-beta.32 and later update themselves. The installer is not code-signed yet:

- Microsoft Defender may report it as `Program:Win32/Contebrew.A!ml`. This is a false positive from its automatic scanner. To keep the file, open Windows Security › Protection history, select the entry and choose **Allow on device**.
- SmartScreen may then say Windows protected your PC. Choose **More info** › **Run anyway**.
- On a PC with **Smart App Control** turned on, Windows may block Retinue, because it is not code-signed. There is no per-app exception; it runs once Smart App Control is off, or once releases are code-signed.
